1. GINO from pinned NeuralOperator commit a6648eeeb60fe0837f2d7d021517a945a159a0c8 can execute the W6 geometry-point encoder and produce a ten-component raw-moment head output on CPU.
2. The GINO adapter's diagnostic coefficient Jacobian executes with torch.func after moving value-dependent validation outside vmap. This is a numerical smoke only; the Jacobian is not admitted to Newton.

## Important scope limits

- These are source integration and CPU smoke tests, not a fair model benchmark.
- GINO/UQNO have no trained Spin weights; QuadrANN is untrained.
- No W2 Cherchi teacher labels were consumed. W2 still has zero admitted training samples and no admitted derivative neighborhood.
- Algoim is an independent numerical reference, not the authoritative Cherchi teacher and not the native Spin Boolean solver.
- No J_D from these adapters is certified or admitted to Newton.
- No Heart, Teapot or Noodles end-to-end speedup, GPU run or 512^3 validation is claimed.
