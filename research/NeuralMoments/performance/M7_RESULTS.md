## Decision: REJECT the candidate decoder optimization

The full four-run, single-host **ABBA** experiment compared standard Python
`json.loads` with optional `orjson.loads` on the **same 25 original archived
Heart H8e Cherchi material packets** (8,455 vertices / 16,910 triangles
per point). The signed H8e archive and independent GMP verifier binary are
unchanged between arms. All 100 packet replays passed original SHA,
construction identity, original numerical reference, strict oriented-boundary
and exact GMP tetrahedron ten-moment gates. A new semantic fingerprint covers
the parsed exact-call geometry/source/backend identity and **binary64 bit
patterns of q, r_D, r_total and the entire 10x10 saved Jacobian**. The same
scientific receipt SHA was obtained in every full 25-point run.

| Actual measurement (25 points per run) | stdlib json | orjson 3.11.9 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Measured wall, samples | 44.677 s; 47.651 s | 46.365 s; 52.100 s |
| Median end-to-end archived validator | **46.164 s** | **49.232 s** |
| Median JSON parse stage | **16.102 s** | **17.639 s** |
| Independent native GMP stage median | 22.278 s | 23.409 s |
